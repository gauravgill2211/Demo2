Framing the Analysis

Among several potential employer wedges, we are exploring where condition-specific health could create a differentiated role for Oura

Recap
  • Employer offerings are converging: plans, navigators and condition vendors are expanding across navigation, virtual care and proactive engagement
  • Navigation / AI-first care faces increasing differentiation pressure: Counsel can extend Oura’s clinical reach, but AI chat and provider access alone are unlikely to create a durable advantage
  • Condition-specific health warrants deeper exploration: it provides a clearer opportunity to connect Oura’s biometric data + engagement to employer health priorities

Right-to-Win Framework

The Oura Right-to-Win Criteria

We first considered the underlying journeys within each health condition, then explored the right-to-win criteria based on those journeys.

1
Data & capability advantage
Question we’re asking
Does Oura already have differentiated data, algorithms, product features or evidence that make it meaningfully better positioned to solve this problem?
2
Can it change a valuable action?
Question we’re asking
Can those capabilities actually change a decision, intervention, escalation or treatment/recovery step that matters economically?
3
Consumer pull
Question we’re asking
Does Oura have the brand permission and ongoing engagement to play a meaningful role in this journey?
4
Competitive whitespace
Question we’re asking
Is there a valuable need that scaled incumbents do not already solve well?
