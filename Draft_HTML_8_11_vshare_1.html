How value flows to Oura
PMPM signal fee
Why:health plans already budget per-member-per-month for population health tools. How:Oura licenses its risk and care-gap signals into an existing care management platform.
Shared savings
Why:risk-bearing providers and ACOs are paid on total cost of care. How:Oura earns a share of the savings tied to earlier detection and better adherence.
Quality incentive
Why:value-based contracts pay bonuses for closing care gaps. How:Oura’s signals help hit the metric, and Oura shares in the resulting incentive payment.
Care management vendor fee
Why:payers already spend heavily on utilization and care management infrastructure. How:Oura is paid as the risk-stratification layer inside an existing program, not the whole infrastructure.

Oura doesn’t need to become the reimbursed provider. It creates value inside a value-based model and captures a share of the economics.

Potential buyers: Health plans · risk-bearing providers · ACOs · Medicare Advantage · population health orgs
